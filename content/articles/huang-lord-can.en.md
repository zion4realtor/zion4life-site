## I cannot.The Lord can.

Before the buildings rose,

$30,000 in daily interest

was already weighing on him.

## He dreamed of a tower.All he had was a hole.

It took two years to secure a loan. Brother Huang recalled paying US$30,000 a day in interest—the price of a Mercedes. “One day’s delay, and another Mercedes was gone!”

Two years of relocation. Two years digging the basement. Then the financial crisis struck. From 1998 to 2000, work stopped completely. He called that enormous pit his greatest “grave” at the time.

> Whenever I ran into a problem, I did not know how to solve it. There was simply no way forward. But I depended on the Body.

## When he saw no way,he did not face it alone.

When he did not know how to go on, he called brothers in Singapore to pray together. He also talked things over with nearby brothers as they jogged.

In 1996, amid relocation difficulties, Brother Lee had told him: “You can do it, but you must pray-read, study, recite, and prophesy!” As he worked, the Lord’s word supplied him inwardly. Work resumed in 2000; the buildings were completed in 2002.

> If I have not touched the Lord today, I have no vitality. If I do not have the brothers and sisters around me, I have no joy.

## I cannot.The Lord can.

Later, one of the hotels bore these words: “I am able to do all things in Him who empowers me.”

In his testimony, he went on to say: “I cannot. The Lord can.”
