## Daily Manna

Genesis, chapter twelve, verse seven. …To your seed I will give this land. And there he built an altar to Jehovah who had appeared to him.

## Living Bread Nourishment

The God of Abraham is the God of the earth. In His second appearing with His third speaking, God promised Abraham that He would give the land of Canaan to his seed. The God of Abraham is the God of secret care for His elect, in saving Abraham from Pharaoh’s insulting of his wife. The God of Abraham is the God of comfort and encouragement, in His fourth speaking, after Lot left Abraham, in promising to give to Abraham and to his seed the land of Canaan as far as he could see in four directions and to make his seed as numerous as the dust of the earth. Christ as the seed of Abraham must be wrought into us so that He may be brought forth through us for His increase. The land of Canaan signifies the all-inclusive Christ as the all-inclusive Spirit in our spirit, enabling us to walk in Him as our all-inclusive land, which is to enjoy His living in us and His dispensing of the riches of the processed Triune God into our being, so that we may be constituted with Him for His corporate expression!

## Responding in Prayer

Oh Lord Jesus! May I know and experience You as the God of this land, the God of secret care for His elect, and the God of comfort and encouragement. Praise You for being the all-inclusive Spirit in my spirit as my good land, enabling me to walk in You, be constituted with You for Your corporate expression!
