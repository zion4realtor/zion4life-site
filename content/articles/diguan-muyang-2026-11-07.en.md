## Daily Manna

Galatians, chapter four, verse twenty-six. But the Jerusalem above is free, which is our mother.

## Living Bread Nourishment

Galatians 4 reveals that the mother of the believers, who is the Jerusalem above, the New Jerusalem is the new covenant of grace symbolized by Sarah. The new covenant is our mother because it brought us forth as children of freedom. Eventually, the mother, Jerusalem from above, is one with the Father. The Father is the grace-giver. However, to receive the new covenant and to keep it is to come to the heavenly Jerusalem and to the church; the new covenant, the heavenly Jerusalem, and the church are one. Galatians deals with Judaism, warning the believers not to backslide into Judaism but to stay in grace. Hebrews charges us not to drift into the old covenant but to remain in the new covenant. God’s intention in choosing Abraham was not simply to restore fallen man to his original, God-created condition but also to produce many sons, brought forth by grace, to constitute the New Jerusalem—His ultimate and eternal corporate expression.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh Lord Jesus! The new covenant of grace is our mother. May we stay in grace not drift into the old covenant. We were brought forth by grace as Your many sons to constitute the New Jerusalem—Your ultimate and eternal corporate expression.
