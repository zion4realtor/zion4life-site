## Daily Manna

Galatians, chapter six, verse sixteen. And as many as walk by this rule, peace be upon them and mercy, even upon the Israel of God.

## Living Bread Nourishment

The issue of experiencing the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob is the Israel of God, which is the church in the Triune God. As the Israel of God, we represent God, exercise His authority, and carry out His administration on earth for the fulfillment of His purpose. When Paul speaks of the church in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, he means that the church is in the Triune God. If we see the vision of the church in the Triune God, we will spontaneously realize that we should not hold to certain concepts or do certain things. We need two kinds of living, two kinds of walk. In the first walk we will have such virtues as love, joy, peace, meekness, and long-suffering, all of which are the expression of the Christ who lives in us. The second kind of walk is to become the Israel of God bearing God’s kingship, representing Him with His authority, and executing His governmental administration. May our eyes be opened to see that we are kings—the Israel of God—and that we need to have another living, a special walk according to the spirit, to execute God’s authority.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh! Lord Jesus! May my eyes be opened to see that we are kings—the Israel of God—and that we need to have a special walk according to the spirit to represent You with Your authority. May I also see that the church is in the Triune God. May this vision govern my thoughts, activities, and my entire living!
