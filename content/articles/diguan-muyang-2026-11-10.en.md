## Daily Manna

Romans, chapter eleven, verse thirty-six. Because out from Him and through Him and to Him are all things. To Him be the glory forever. Amen.

## Living Bread Nourishment

God tested Abraham by instructing him to take his only son, Isaac, and offer him as a burnt offering. The burnt offering typifies Christ not mainly in His redeeming man from sin but in His living a life that is perfect and absolutely for God and for God’s satisfaction and in His being the life that enables God’s people to have such a living. Isaac’s growing, living, and calling on the name of El Olam at Beer-sheba are all for the building up of a burnt offering that he might be burned on the altar on Mount Moriah. The more we drink the water from the well of Beer-sheba, the more we will grow, and the more we grow, the more we will be prepared for the fire on Mount Moriah. The proper church life produces burnt offering. The more we stay in the church life, the more it will bring us from Beer-sheba to Moriah. Here we see a basic principle in God’s economy: all that God has given us, even what He has wrought into us and through us, must eventually be offered back to Him, that we may live a life of faith, not holding on to anything, but relying only on Him.

## Responding in Prayer

Lord Jesus, may I simply depend on You. Everything You have given me, even what You have wrought into me and through me, I offer back to You. Cause me to remain in the proper church life, drink You as the living water, call on Your name, grow in Your life, and be prepared as a burnt offering to God, so that You may be satisfied!
