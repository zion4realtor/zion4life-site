## Daily Manna

Hebrews, chapter twelve, verse eleven. Now no discipline at the present time seems to be a matter of joy, but of grief; but afterward it yields the peaceable fruit of righteousness to those who have been exercised by it.

## Living Bread Nourishment

Jacob’s history signifies the discipline of the Holy Spirit. The God of Jacob—the God of dealings—implies how the Holy Spirit disciplined Jacob, dealt with his natural life, and constituted Christ into him. The discipline of the Holy Spirit refers to what the Holy Spirit is doing in our outward environment—to His arranging of all people, things, and happenings—through which we are disciplined, the element of the old creation in us is completely torn down, so that the element of the new creation may be built up in us. It is so easy for us to make proposals and to act without prayer. One day the Lord will come to deal with our natural life; then we will become humble and follow the Lord in fear and trembling. This is the common trait of all those who know God: every time we walk, we realize our lameness and our weakness. God deals with our natural life through the discipline of the Holy Spirit so that Christ may be wrought into us, constituted into us, and formed in us for the corporate expression of the Triune God.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh, Lord Jesus! Cause me to experience You as the God of Jacob—the God of dealings. May I experience the Holy Spirit’s discipline in me, accept Your arrangement of all people, things, and happenings, and allow the Holy Spirit to work in me to deal with my natural life, so that You may be wrought into me, constituted into me, and formed in me.
