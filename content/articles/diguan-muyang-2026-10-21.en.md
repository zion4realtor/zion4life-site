## Daily Manna

Romans, chapter four, verses sixteen, second part, to seventeen. Abraham, who is the father of us all (As it is written, "I have appointed you a father of many nations") in the sight of God whom he believed, who gives life to the dead and calls the things not being as being.

## Living Bread Nourishment

Abraham believed in God as the unique source, as the One who “calls the things not being as being”. Abraham had no thought of becoming God’s people. It was God who initiated and brought him from the other side of the Euphrates River. God did a special work in Abraham in order to show him what it means for God to be the Father—the unique initiator, and that everything originates from Him. The day that God shows you that He is the Father will be a blessed day. On that day you will realize that you cannot do anything instead you will ask, “Has God initiated this?” Isaac is the best figure of the Son. In him we see that everything comes from the Father and our place is to receive. To know the God of Isaac is to know God as the supplier. In the eyes of God, we are only those who receive. The principle of receiving is the principle of Isaac. Our salvation, victory, justification, sanctification, forgiveness, and freedom are all the result of receiving. We need to see that God is the Father, from whom everything proceeds from Him; we are sons, and everything we have is from Him.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh! Lord Jesus! Cause me to know and experience You as the God of Abraham—You are the unique source, that I may not initiate anything from myself, but rather let God originate everything. Also cause me to know and experience You as the God of Isaac—God is the Supplier, learning to remain in the position of receiving, to receive everything from God!
