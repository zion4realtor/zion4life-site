## Daily Manna

Acts, chapter seven, verses two to four. …The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham… And said to him, "Come out from your land and from your relatives, and come into the land which I will show you." Then he came forth from the land of the Chaldeans and dwelt in Haran. And from there, after his father died, He removed him into this land, in which you now dwell.

## Living Bread Nourishment

God unveiled what kind of God He is in His dealings with Abraham. The God of Abraham is the God of glory. This is shown in His first appearing with His first calling and His first speaking to Abraham, where God became the vast magnet and the great motivation that moved Abraham to come out of the country of satanic idolatry. When God called Abraham, He did not tell him where he should go. Moment after moment and step after step, Abraham’s heart had to cleave to God and trust in God for every move. Believing in God means to terminate ourselves and to let God be our very being, and to allow God to be everything in us. When we are called by God in this way, the living God transfuses Himself into us and causes us to react to God by believing in Him. This reaction is faith. Faith does not originate with us; it comes from God. Faith is not separate from Christ, for it is actually Christ Himself transfusing Himself into us and producing a reaction within us. Once we have this transfusion, we will experience a spiritual infusion as God’s essence infiltrates our being.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh, Lord Jesus! Praise You as the God of glory, who appeared to Abraham and called him. Today, You personally draw us, transfusing Yourself into us as the element of faith. I long to experience more of this spiritual infusion, allowing You to saturate and fill my entire being to be everything in me!
