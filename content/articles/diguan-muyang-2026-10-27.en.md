## Daily Manna

Galatians, chapter three, verse fourteen. In order that the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.

## Living Bread Nourishment

The God of Abraham is the God of blessing, in His second calling with His second speaking to Abraham, promising him that He would make of him a great nation, make his name great, and make him a blessing to others so that all the families of the earth would be blessed in him; this attracted Abraham to go into the good land of the divine promise. According to Galatians 3:14, the blessing promised here is actually God Himself as the Spirit; the greatest blessing is the Triune God—the Father, Son, and Spirit—as the processed, all-inclusive, life-giving Spirit dwelling in us in a most subjective way for our enjoyment. Through the process of crucifixion and resurrection, Christ became the life-giving Spirit, the compound Spirit compounded with Christ’s divinity, His humanity, His human living, His all-inclusive death with its effectiveness, His powerful resurrection with its life power, and His ascension. Oh, what a blessing that we can enjoy such an all-inclusive One as our daily portion.

## Responding in Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank You for passing through the process of crucifixion and resurrection to become the all-inclusive, life-giving Spirit, as the blessing of Abraham, coming into me to be my daily enjoyment.
