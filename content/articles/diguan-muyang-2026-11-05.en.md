## Daily Manna

Colossians, chapter two, verse eleven. In Him also you were circumcised with a circumcision not made with hands, in the putting off of the body of the flesh, in the circumcision of Christ.

## Living Bread Nourishment

The covenant that God made with Abraham in Genesis 15 was confirmed in chapter 17 with circumcision. In verse 1, God revealed Himself to Abraham as the All-sufficient God, the source of grace to bring forth Christ as the seed for the fulfillment of His purpose. In Genesis 16, Abraham exercised his flesh to produce Ishmael; in Genesis 17, God charged Abraham to cut off his flesh, to terminate his natural strength, so that God could come in and bring forth Isaac by His grace. The spiritual meaning of circumcision is to put off the flesh, to put off the self and old man, through the crucifixion of Christ. As long as our natural strength remains, it is difficult for God to come in to be our everything for the fulfillment of His purpose. We all must declare that our old man, the self, and natural strength have been crucified already and live according to this fact; then the God of resurrection immediately has the way to come into us and to be everything to us for the carrying out of His economy. Whenever we are unwilling to be circumcised, we are finished with the fulfilling of God’s eternal purpose.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh, Lord Jesus! I am willing to declare that my self and my old man have been crucified and to live according to this fact, so that my natural strength may be terminated and the God of resurrection may have a way to enter into me. May I experience You as the All-sufficient God, the source of grace, and as everything to me, so that Your purpose may be accomplished!
