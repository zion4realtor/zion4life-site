## Daily Manna

Exodus, chapter three, verse fifteen. And God also said to Moses, Thus you shall say to the children of Israel, Jehovah, the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you. This is My name forever…

## Living Bread Nourishment

The Bible shows us that God’s people had two beginnings. The first beginning was with Abraham because God’s selection and calling began with Abraham. The other beginning was with the nation of Israel. God told the Israelites that they would be a people to Him among all the nations. They would be a kingdom of priests. Between these two beginnings, God has obtained these three persons, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who are the foundations of the nation of Israel; without them there would not be the nation of Israel. All true Israelites must say that Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob are their ancestors. In Abraham, we see God the Father; in Isaac, we see God the Son; and in Jacob, we see God the Spirit. They are a complete person in the experience of life. Abraham did not bless, because he did not have the maturity in life. Isaac, having some maturity but lacking the richness of the maturity of life, blessed in a blind way. Jacob, who was mature in life, blessed in a full and clear way. Eventually, Jacob became Israel, the expression of God!

## Responding in Prayer

Oh, Lord Jesus! Praise You for being the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob! I am willing to know and experience more of these three aspects so that I may reach maturity in life and become an Israel who can bless others!
