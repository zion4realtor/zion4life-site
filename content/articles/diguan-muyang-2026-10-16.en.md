## Daily Manna

Song of Songs, chapter six, verse four. You are as beautiful, my love, as Tirzah, as lovely as Jerusalem, as terrible as an army with banners.

## Living Bread Nourishment

When the overcoming lover of Christ becomes one with God to be God’s dwelling place, she is as beautiful as Tirzah and as lovely as Jerusalem in the eyes of God. However, to the enemy this lover is as terrible as an army with banners. Banners indicate a readiness to fight and also symbols of victory. The lovers of Christ should be lovable and terrible at the same time; however, many believers have lost their loveliness before the Lord and their terribleness before the enemy. An army fights the battle for God’s kingdom in the degradation of God’s people to be the overcomers answering the Lord’s call. The overcoming lover of Christ not only has a future that is full of hope and a life that is absolutely heavenly, but is also a victor who constantly sings the triumphant song. The enemy is frightened by the church that is built up as the city of God. A terrible army signifies that these overcomers of the Lord terrify God’s enemy, Satan. Satan is not afraid of individualistic Christians, even if they number in the thousands, but is terrified of the church as the Body of Christ, the corporate warrior fighting against him and his kingdom.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh! Lord Jesus! I desire to be built up into the church—the city of God. I answer Your call to be an overcomer, as terrible as an army with banners. May I be lovely in Your eyes, yet terrible and frightening to Your enemy, Satan!
