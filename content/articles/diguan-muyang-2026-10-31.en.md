## Daily Manna

Genesis, chapter twenty-two, verses thirteen to fourteen. And Abraham lifted up his eyes and looked, and there behind him was a ram, caught in the thicket by its horns. And Abraham went and took the ram and offered it up for a burnt offering in place of his son. And Abraham called the name of that place Jehovah-jireh, as it is said to this day, On the mount of Jehovah it will be provided.

## Living Bread Nourishment

The God of Abraham is the God with His human friendship. He conversed with Abraham as a human friend with a human friend, concerning His judgment of Sodom. He remembered His intimate friend Abraham, brought Abraham’s nephew and his two daughters out from the overthrow of Sodom, and rescued his wife from falling into the hands of Abimelech, king of Gerar. The God of Abraham is the God of changelessness in keeping the principle of His grace, in recognizing Isaac, his son of Sarah, as his only son. He is the God with His trial by asking Abraham to present his only son, whom he loved, as a burnt offering to God. He is also the Triune God of provision, providing a ram to replace Isaac for a burnt offering to God. Moreover, He is the Triune God of blessing, promising and blessing Abraham that in his seed (Christ) would all the nations of the earth be blessed. As a whole, the God of Abraham is the God of speaking in His appearing, with calling, in a vision, and in the human friendship, to unveil to His intimate friend on the earth for the accomplishment of the eternal economy for the Divine Trinity.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh! Lord Jesus! Praise You for being the God of friendship in humanity, the almighty God of care, the unchanging God, and the Triune God of blessing. May I be Your intimate friend on earth, experiencing Your appearing, calling, and hearing You speak to me in visions and in human friendship.
