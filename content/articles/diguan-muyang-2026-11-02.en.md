## Daily Manna

Galatians, chapter four, verses twenty-two to twenty-four. For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one of the maidservant and one of the free woman. However the one of the maidservant was born according to the flesh, but the one of the free woman was born through promise. These things are spoken allegorically, for these women are two covenants…

## Living Bread Nourishment

Sarah and Hagar, the wife and the concubine of Abraham, are an allegory of two covenants—the covenant of promise and the covenant of law. Sarah, the free woman, signifies the covenant of promise, which is related to the New Testament, the covenant of grace. In that covenant God promised that He would give Abraham the seed, without having any intention that Abraham needed to do anything in order to have it; God would work something into him so that he might bring forth a seed to fulfill His purpose; this is grace. The produce of the promise of grace, which is Isaac, is the seed for the fulfillment of God’s purpose. If we want Christ to inherit the land, we should not try to bring Him in by ourselves. This is the greatest test. We must remember that God is not only asking how well a work is done, but who is doing the work. Abraham was wrong, not in his goal but in his source. The principle of promise is totally different from the principle of Ishmael. May the Lord be merciful to us and deliver us from the principle of Ishmael.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh Lord Jesus! Praise You for being the source of everything, the God who calls things not being as being. I am willing to learn to let go of myself, allowing more of Your element to be wrought into me that I may enjoy You as grace in the principle of promise.
