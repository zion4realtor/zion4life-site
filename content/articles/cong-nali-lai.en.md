At some point in life, everyone runs into three questions: Who am I? Where do I come from? Where am I going?

## A simple comparison

To know what something is for, ask the one who made it. Why a pen is shaped the way it is, the pen maker can tell you best.

People are the same. Why we are alive is best asked of the One who made us.
