## Daily Manna

Romans, chapter eight, verse twenty-eight. And we know that all things work together for good to those who love God, to those who are called according to His purpose.

## Living Bread Nourishment

In the book of Genesis the records of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob overlap; Genesis does not portray them as three separate individuals but as constituents of one corporate person with three sections. The experience of Abraham signifies the experience of God the Father, the unique source, in His calling man, justifying man, and equipping man to live by faith and to live in fellowship with Him. The experience of Isaac signifies the experience of God the Son in His redeeming man and His blessing man with the inheritance of all His riches, with a life of the enjoyment of His abundance, and with a life in peace. The experience of Jacob (with Joseph) signifies the experience of God the Father in His loving man and choosing man and of God the Spirit in His causing all things to work together for the good of those who love Him, in His transforming man, and in His making man mature in the divine life so that man may be able to bless all the people, rule over all the earth, and satisfy all the people with God the Son as the life supply.

## Responding in Prayer

Lord Jesus, may I experience You as the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob by experiencing You in the three aspects of the Father, the Son, and the Spirit, so that I may grow to maturity in the divine life, bless all the people, and reign with You over the whole earth!
