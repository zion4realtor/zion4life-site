## Daily Manna

Genesis, chapter twenty-two, verses one to two. Now after these things God tested Abraham and said to him, Abraham. And he said, Here I am. And He said, Take now your son, your only son, whom you love, Isaac, and go to the land of Moriah, and offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains of which I will tell you.

## Living Bread Nourishment

After the mention of Abraham’s planting a tamarisk tree and calling on the name of Jehovah, God came in to test him. He received the highest demand from God—to give back to God what God had given him. Often after we have had the best enjoyment of the Lord, He will not ask us to do something for Him; rather, He will tell us to offer back to Him what He has given us. We always expect that after having a good time with the Lord, He will command us to do something for Him. We never imagine that He may ask us to give back to Him that which He has given us. Many Christians have never learned this lesson of taking their hands off the work that God has given them. Nevertheless, all that God has given us—including, power, work, and success, even what He has wrought in and through us—must be offered back to Him. This is the real test and this is the way we all must take today.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh Lord Jesus! Praise You as the God who raises the dead. I am willing to learn more to lay down all my work, abilities, and success, and even to offer back to You what You have given me!
