## Daily Manna

Genesis, chapter twenty-two, verses seventeen to eighteen. I will surely bless you and will greatly multiply your seed like the stars of the heavens and like the sand which is on the seashore; and your seed shall possess the gate of his enemies. And in your seed all the nations of the earth shall be blessed, because you have obeyed My voice.

## Living Bread Nourishment

By being offered back to God by Abraham, Isaac was multiplied to become the New Jerusalem. The New Jerusalem will be the ultimate consummation of Abraham’s seed—the sand (the twelve tribes of Israel representing the Old Testament saints) and the stars (representing the twelve apostles of the New Testament). These two peoples eventually will be built together into the eternal New Jerusalem. This is God’s blessing to Abraham. This blessing is not a blessing of material things, but a blessing of multiplication. Here we see a basic principle—that the way to have God’s gift multiplied is to offer back to God what He has given to us. If Abraham had not offered Isaac back to God, he might have had only one Isaac. But having been offered back to God, one Isaac was multiplied into numberless stars and sand. If we offer our one grain to God and allow Him to put it to death, it will be returned to us in resurrection, and we will see multiplication under God’s blessing.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh! Lord Jesus! We praise You for the blessing You have given us—the blessing of multiplication! I am willing to offer my one grain to God and let God put it to death. Then it will be returned to us in resurrection, and we will see multiplication under God’s blessing!
