## Daily Manna

Galatians, chapter five, verse one. It is for freedom that Christ has set us free; stand fast therefore, and do not be entangled with a yoke of slavery again.

## Living Bread Nourishment

Grace, like Sarah, is the proper wife, the mother of the proper heirs, bringing forth children unto sonship. Hagar, Abraham’s concubine, is a symbol of the law; by this we can see that the position of the law is the position of a concubine, as such, the law has no rightful position in God’s economy. Mount Sinai is the place where the law was given. The covenant of law, symbolized by Hagar, brings God’s chosen people into the bondage of the law, making them slaves under the law, separated from the grace of God. Abraham’s producing of Ishmael through Hagar symbolizes man’s attempt to fulfill God’s purpose by the effort of the flesh in coordination with the law. Ishmael, as the issue of man’s fleshly effort according to the law, was rejected by God. All of us might focus on God’s intention but neglect the grace. God’s intention is that we live a heavenly life for the glory of God, and the grace means that God will work Christ into us to live this heavenly life of pleasing God and glorifying God for us for the fulfillment of His purpose.

## Responding in Prayer

Lord Jesus, may I desire not only God's purpose but also be preserved from being under the covenant of the law symbolized by Hagar. Keep me from using my own fleshly efforts to cooperate with the law. Instead, keep me in the position of grace, allowing You to work Yourself into me and live out the heavenly life for me, so that God's purpose may be fulfilled!
