## Daily Manna

Galatians, chapter five, verse four. You have been brought to nought, separated from Christ, you who are being justified by law; you have fallen from grace.

## Living Bread Nourishment

“The promise” God gave to Abraham is in Genesis 12:2,7; 13:15-17; and 15:4-5, and “the covenant” was made in 15:7-21. According to God’s intention, the covenant of promise came first. God had no intention of bringing in the law and of having man endeavor to keep it for the fulfillment of His purpose. What He originally intended to do was to work Himself into man to fulfill His purpose through man. According to the New Testament, the proper fruit bearing and gospel preaching are by the overflow of the inner life, by God working Christ into, through, and out of us. Blessed is the one who stops trying to live a Christian life by himself or to work for the Lord by worldly means. The produce of the effort of the flesh was Ishmael. Our Ishmael breaks our fellowship with God and keeps us from His appearing. We must forget our doing and our working and take care of God’s appearing. When God’s appearing is with us, we are in the grace, in the covenant of grace.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh, Lord Jesus! May I not live the Christian life by myself or work for You by worldly means. Instead, may I cease my own efforts, remain in fellowship with You, forget my own doing and working, simply enjoy You as grace, and care only for Your appearing and presence!
