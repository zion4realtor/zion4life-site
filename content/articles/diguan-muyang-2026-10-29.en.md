## Daily Manna

Genesis, chapter fifteen, verses five to six. And He brought him outside and said, Look now toward the heavens, and count the stars, if you are able to count them. And He said to him, So shall your seed be. And he believed Jehovah, and He accounted it to him as righteousness.

## Living Bread Nourishment

The God of Abraham is God the Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth, in His divine supply with His blessing to Abraham through His priest Melchizedek after Abraham fought the battle against the four kings. The God of Abraham is the God of shield and exceedingly great reward, in His fifth speaking in a vision to Abraham, when he was afraid of the four kings’ avenging, in pointing out to Abraham that his heavenly seed would be as many as the stars in heaven. Abraham believed in Jehovah, and Jehovah accounted it to him as righteousness. The God of Abraham is the God of faithfulness, in foretelling to Abraham, in His sixth speaking to him in his deep sleep, that his earthly seed would be sojourners in Egypt, serve the Egyptians, be afflicted by them for four hundred years, and in their fourth generation return to Canaan, and the faithful God would give to his seed the land from the river of Egypt to the great river of Euphrates. The God of Abraham is the God of silence. Because Abraham got a son by his flesh, God became silent to Abraham for thirteen years, until he became ninety-nine years old, and God appeared to him again!

## Responding in Prayer

Oh Lord Jesus! Praise You that You are the God of Abraham, the Most High God who brings the divine supply. You are the God of exceedingly great reward, the faithful God, who justify me by faith. May I experience You more and more through Your continual speaking, and may You never be silent toward me.
