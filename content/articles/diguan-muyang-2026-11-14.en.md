## Daily Manna

Philippians, chapter three, verse ten. To know Him and the power of His resurrection and the fellowship of His sufferings, being conformed to His death.

## Living Bread Nourishment

Abraham was the first to worship God with the burnt offering on Mount Zion. God commanded Abraham’s descendants, the children of Israel, to go three times a year to this place to worship God and there to offer to Him their burnt offerings. Abraham’s spiritual descendants, the New Testament believers, are on Mount Zion. In our spiritual experience, Mount Moriah eventually becomes Mount Zion. The Lord’s recovery is to build up Zion—the reality of the Body of Christ consummating in the New Jerusalem. To be in the reality of the Body of Christ, we need to be absolutely in the resurrection life of Christ. We do have some good coordination in the local churches. However, is this kind of coordination carried out by the natural life or in resurrection? To be in resurrection means that our natural life is crucified, and then the God-created part of our being is uplifted in resurrection to be one with Christ in resurrection. We need to endeavor to reach in the church life the highest peak, today’s Zion, which is the reality of the Body of Christ until we consummate in the New Jerusalem.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh, Lord Jesus! I am willing to follow in the footsteps of Abraham and offer burnt offerings to God on Mount Moriah, that is, Mount Zion, so that my coordination in the church life may be in the resurrection life. May I endeavor to reach the highest peak of the reality of the Body of Christ in the church life, which is today’s Zion!
