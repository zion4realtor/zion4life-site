## Daily Manna

Ephesians, chapter six, verses ten to eleven. Finally, be empowered in the Lord and in the might of His strength. Put on the whole armor of God that you may be able to stand against the stratagems of the devil.

## Living Bread Nourishment

The church is seen in chapter 6 of Ephesians as a warrior putting on the whole armor of God for the defeating of God’s enemy. In the universe there are three wills: the divine will, the satanic will, and the human will. It is because of God’s greatness that He gave man a free will. By giving man a free will, God was indicating that He would not force man to obey Him. In Genesis 2 we see that man was free to exercise his will to eat either of the tree of life or of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. These two trees represent the divine will and the satanic will, respectively. Hence, in the garden there was a triangular situation: God, Satan, and man—each one with a will. Through repentance man can turn from the satanic will to the divine will. Spiritual warfare is necessary because Satan’s will is set against God’s will. As the church, our fighting is to subdue the satanic will and to defeat God’s enemy. Our spiritual walk is for the fulfillment of God’s purpose, our living is for the satisfaction of Christ, and our warfare is for the defeat of God’s enemy and to maintain His victory.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh! Lord Jesus! Preserve my Christian life, for the satisfaction of Christ, and to share in the spiritual warfare, choosing God's will with my will, defeating Satan's will, and maintaining Your victory!
