## Daily Manna

Exodus, chapter three, verses fourteen to fifteen. And God said to Moses, I AM WHO I AM; … the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has sent me to you. This is My name forever, and this is My memorial from generation to generation.

## Living Bread Nourishment

In Exodus 3:15 God Himself said that He is the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob and He also revealed that He is Jehovah Elohim. Elohim means “the faithful mighty One.” Jehovah means “He who was, who is, and who is to be.” The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob is the eternal great I Am. The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob is the God of the tabernacle. Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob each lived in a tent; while they were living in tents, they were eagerly waiting for the eternal tabernacle of God, the city of New Jerusalem. The Triune God embodied in the Son was incarnated to be His tabernacle on the earth. By His incarnation He mingled God with man to be a mutual abode of God and man. The tabernacle of God was first the individual God-man Jesus and ultimately consummates in the corporate New Jerusalem. In eternity future the New Jerusalem will be the enlargement of Christ, the eternal mingling of God with man, and the mutual abode of God and man.

## Responding in Prayer

Oh! Lord Jesus! Praise You that You are the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and the eternal great I Am. You are also the God of the tabernacle, who through incarnation made God and man a mutual abode. Today, we dwell in tents, eagerly waiting for the eternal tabernacle of God, which is the New Jerusalem!
