## Today’s Prayer

Lord Jesus, we praise You! Thank You for revealing to us in this age the God-ordained way. This is not an outward method of work; it is the way according to the Bible for all the saints to function and for the Body of Christ to be built up organically.

Lord, forgive us for hearing much truth but lacking practical exercise; for attending many meetings but not bearing and nourishing fruit normally; and for knowing that everyone is a priest yet still depending on a few to fulfill the ministry.

Lord, renew us day by day and renew our vision of the four steps of the God-ordained way: begetting people through preaching the gospel, nourishing them in home meetings, perfecting them in group meetings, and building them up through prophesying in the church meetings.

O Lord Jesus, these four steps may seem to make demands on us. Yet if we do not practice them, it will be difficult for us to remain in the Lord’s recovery and to be functioning members of the Body of Christ.

O brothers and sisters, this is the issue: What kind of Christians should we be today—overcomers or defeated ones?

Lord, raise up the vital groups! Cause us to be no longer individualistic or passive, but to seek companions, pray thoroughly, open to one another, and be in one accord, becoming living and effective members.

Brothers and sisters, we cannot merely establish the framework of the vital groups; we must also enrich them in content. Our practice of the vital groups is for the overcomers to save the church from degradation. If the vital groups fail, the Lord will have no way to do anything.

May every vital group become a place for mutual intercession, mutual shepherding, mutual teaching, and mutual perfecting! May the new ones be nourished, the weak be cherished, the dormant recover their function, and everyone supply Christ.

Lord, do not let us be satisfied with meetings without a living, knowledge without practice, or a beginning without continuance. Cause us to exercise daily, labor week by week, and steadfastly take the God-ordained way.

May the new ones we beget continue to beget others, the saints we shepherd continue to shepherd, and the members we perfect continue to perfect others, so that the vital groups may continually multiply.

Lord, gain a company of overcomers! May the vital groups prevail throughout the earth, all the saints function normally, the Body of Christ be built up organically, and the bride be prepared to welcome Your return.

Lord Jesus, we consecrate ourselves to take the way You have ordained until You return. Amen!

## Ministry Fellowship

### The four major steps in the practice of the new way

This time we want to consider practically the new way that we arrived at through more than four years of study and research. After all this study and investigation, we have to admit that the four major steps in this way are: first, begetting people through the preaching of the gospel; second, nourishing people through the home meetings; third, perfecting people through the small group meetings; and fourth, building people up through prophesying in the larger meetings. This is the way revealed in the Bible and ordained by the Lord. Thank the Lord! After these four years, the church in Taipei was put on the right track and set on this proper way. These four major steps form the structure for our meeting, worship, service, and living for the Lord. Simply put, this structure consists of one kind of gospel preaching and three kinds of meetings—the home, group, and district meetings. However, although this structure is now in place, its content still needs to be greatly enriched. We are dissatisfied with our former way because it did not adequately cultivate the saints in begetting, nourishing, and teaching. Our former practice was too vague and general. Now, when a brother or sister has even a little desire after being saved, we can lead him or her step by step from begetting to nourishing and from nourishing to teaching. Teaching is the perfecting spoken of in Ephesians 4:12. Those with gifts should not mainly do the work themselves—for example, an evangelist should not mainly preach the gospel himself. Their main work is to perfect the saints to do what they themselves can do. This is like a university professor, whose main task is not to practice his specialty directly but to perfect his students at the university. After several years, the students graduate and can do what the professor does. This is what we hope for. But in the past, when the word was preached among us, everyone listened passively, and people were not perfected. For decades we have promoted the universal priesthood because we saw in the Bible that every saved person is a priest, yet we have not carried this out. (*The Organic Practice of the New Way*, ch. 2)

### The four steps every Christian practices in the new way

Our daily duty is to practice these four steps of the new way: knock on doors and preach the gospel to beget spiritual children—everyone should do this. Once the children are born, everyone should nourish them. Nourishing is not enough; we must also teach. After teaching, we must perfect them; after perfecting them, they must prophesy. These four steps may seem to place demands on us, but if we do not practice them, it will be difficult for us to remain in the Lord’s recovery and to be functioning members of the Body of Christ. This is the issue: What kind of Christians do you want to be today—overcomers or defeated ones? (*The New Testament Priests of the Gospel*, ch. 2)

### Contacting people makes us vital

I believe the way to help us become more vital is to contact people. If we keep waiting and do not contact people, we will never become vital. Each week we should set aside one day to contact people. In addition to the regular meetings of the church, we also need to set aside one evening each week for a vital group meeting. We should still attend the Lord’s Day morning meeting and the prayer meeting. Each week we must set aside one day to gain people. If we are burdened to recover someone who has not attended the meetings for a long time, we need to pray for him, perhaps even fasting and praying. Then we should receive the Lord’s leading to contact him, either by phone or by visiting him. If we are determined to gain people, there will be a way to gain them. We believe this. We must ask the Lord to give us people to gain for Him. We cannot be idle. We must gain those who are not yet vital and bear the burden to make them vital. Then we will see that we ourselves must first become vital. After contacting all the members who are not yet vital, including those who have backslidden, we can begin to contact sinners, including our relatives, neighbors, and colleagues. We must pray and ask the Lord to give us people on whom we can labor. If we have this desire, the Lord will give us the right people. As we take action with the Lord to contact people, we need to pray desperately. We should tell the Lord that we do not want to be dormant, nor do we want others in the church to be dormant. We need the Lord to make us vital, and then go to make others vital. (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, ch. 24)

### Pray desperately for the vital groups

If we are going to have vital groups, we need to be vital. We need to pray desperately, saying, “Lord, we ask You, when the season comes, to give each of us two persons as remaining fruit. Otherwise, we cannot go on.” We should pray again and again until the Lord answers our prayer. We should not be disappointed or discouraged; rather, we should have full assurance that the Lord will fulfill His word. He has told us that He chose and appointed us to go forth, bear fruit, and have fruit that remains.

If you have this kind of burdened prayer, each time you pray you will gain the Spirit. You will receive the Spirit filling you within and the Spirit poured out upon you without. Our meetings should not be empty performances but meetings filled with the Spirit. Our vital group training meetings are not ordinary meetings; they are for the overcomers to save the church from degradation. If the vital groups fail, the Lord will have no way to do anything. (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, ch. 23)
