## Today’s Prayer Burden

Thorough service and consecration fill us with joy. We cannot be Christians halfway; we need to be thorough, full, and absolute. The Lord urgently needs corporate overcomers to be His testimony. His recovery needs a revival of morale, and a revival of the powerful energy and impact within us. The churches need effective increase, and all the saints need to be living and vigorous. May the Lord gain us to hold nothing back, but to hand ourselves over to Him and become a way for Him to advance on earth.

## Today’s Prayer

Lord, raise us up as a people who from now on are willing to follow You thoroughly, desperately, and without reserve, forsaking all. May our consecration encourage many others to offer themselves too. Show the brothers and sisters that mammon and the world cannot help us; they enslave and damage us. Lord, we do not belong to this world; we belong to You. You shed Your blood on the cross for the gospel. Make us willing to spend ourselves for the gospel—to sweat, deny ourselves, and even suffer.

Lord, may Your shining face appear to us and teach us to forsake all. Change our valuation and view of everything. Make us blind to the world and mammon, yet give us a glorious appreciation of You Yourself and of Your gospel. Lord, have mercy on us. Amen.

Lord Jesus, thank You for choosing us. We are willing to consecrate ourselves thoroughly to You and be absolute for You. In these last days before Your return, make us the corporate overcomers You desire, a beachhead through which You can return to possess the earth. Lord, we are willing to be trampled by You. Make us living and full of impact, well pleasing to You. The question today is not merely whether we have the church life, but whether it is adequate. Make us thorough, full, and absolute Christians, a testimony to You. Revive the morale in Your recovery, grant the churches effective increase, and make all the saints living and vigorous!

## Ministry Fellowship

### 1. The overcomers typified by Zion are a beachhead for the Lord to possess the earth

The overcomers typified by Zion are a beachhead and stepping-stone for the Lord to return and possess the earth (Psa. 48:2; Dan. 2:34–35). Some brothers recently visited Normandy in France, where the Allied forces landed during World War II. Their visit impressed on them how important it is in warfare to establish a beachhead. After the Allies secured one, they built an artificial harbor to bring in people, supplies, and vehicles and advance inland.

When the Lord gains overcomers in the church as His beachhead, more supply of life and anointing will follow. It is the privilege of the overcomers to be the Lord’s beachhead and stepping-stone, through whom He can return to possess the earth. May we all become such overcomers so that the Lord can establish His kingdom on earth. (Based on the 2025 Winter Training, Message 5.)

### 2. The four urgent needs in the Lord’s recovery

We must admit that the deadness of Sardis and the lukewarmness of Laodicea can also enter into the Lord’s recovery. We should condemn the dead condition, hate the lukewarm condition, and come out of them. This is not a small matter; it concerns the life or death of the entire recovery. We all need to be living and vigorous.

There are four needs today. First, the Lord needs corporate overcomers to be His genuine testimony. Second, the Lord’s recovery needs a revival of morale, impact, powerful energy, and vitality. Third, the churches need effective growth, multiplication, and increase. Fourth, all of us in the Lord’s recovery urgently need to be living and vigorous. Through intimate and thorough fellowship, confession, consecration, prayer, the saturation of our whole being with the Spirit, labor, and learning, we need to be enlivened. We should pray, “Lord, make me living and vigorous. For Your move, make me living and vigorous today.”

We therefore need to thoroughly reconsider the way we have been taking. The Lord has fully revealed His God-ordained way through His ministry, yet we may have become somewhat numb in our response to His speaking and continue according to habit and tradition. May the Lord touch us afresh through His word and bring us onto His ordained way. (Based on *The Lord’s Recovery and Our Present Need*, Message 7.)

### 3. Through thorough consecration, we become a way for the Lord

Before the Lord entered Jerusalem, people broke off branches and took off their garments to spread them on the road for His glorious entry. For the gospel to go out today, we also need to offer ourselves as a way for the Lord. It is not enough to lay down branches and garments; we ourselves must be handed over. If we hold something back and refuse to offer ourselves, the Lord has no way in us. The Lord must be able to pass through us; then the gospel can reach more people through us.

For the Lord and His gospel, we should hold nothing back or calculate the cost. May the Lord keep us from being entangled by the world and make us thorough, joyful Christians. Those who are unwilling to consecrate themselves often go away sorrowful; the more complete our consecration, the more joy we have within. Thorough service is not doing a little reluctantly; it is handing ourselves over to the Lord so that He may have a way in us. May we become more thorough day by day and be filled with joy within. (Based on *Matured Leadings in the Lord’s Recovery*, ch. 8, “A Special Fellowship—Handing Over,” and ch. 9, “Forsaking All to Serve God.”)
