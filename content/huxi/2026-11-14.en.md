## Today’s Prayer Burden

In the 1980s, as Brother Lee led the churches to change their practice and take the new way, he was willing to “wait”: the elders and co-workers would lead, the churches would begin together, and the saints would gradually catch up. But in 1992, when he fellowshipped concerning the vital groups, he said that we should “no longer wait.” If even one person would turn directly to the Lord in desperate prayer, the practice of the vital groups could begin. He saw that waiting during the change in practice had not brought a real breakthrough; if we kept waiting, we would never become vital! The 2022 Summer Training again called us to see that when the majority are lukewarm, God raises up overcomers to bear a contrary testimony. Even if there is only one, stand faithfully like Elijah and Antipas. No longer wait—begin with me!

## Today’s Prayer

### 1. Lord, show us the serious turn from waiting in the 1980s to no longer waiting in the 1990s

Lord Jesus, shake us! In the 1980s, as Brother Lee led the change in practice, he was willing to wait—to wait for the elders to take the lead, for the co-workers to enter in, and for the churches as a whole to follow. But in the 1990s he saw that if we kept waiting, we would never become vital. O Lord, more than thirty years have passed. How much longer will we wait? We wait for everyone to rise up, for an arrangement to move things forward, and for someone else to go first. In the end, we have waited until we ourselves have become old, and the church life has become routine. Lord, let us hear this turning point: from waiting for everyone to begin to one person rising up first; from depending on others to move us to turning directly to You in desperate prayer. No longer wait—today, begin with me!

### 2. Lord, make me vital first; let me practice the vital groups and build up the Body organically

Lord Jesus, You do not need to wait for the whole church to rise up before the vital groups can begin. If even one person is willing to open thoroughly to You, confess, consecrate himself, pray desperately, and be filled with the Spirit, You can gain a vital beginning. O Lord, save us from speaking about vitality without being vital, and hoping for revival without rising up. Begin with me: make me a vital person. Send me to contact another seeking one. Begin with two people and produce a genuine vital group. Lord, do not let us spend more time waiting. In prayer, contacting, and shepherding, enliven one saint after another. While others wait for someone to kindle the fire, kindle me first; while we wait for the whole church to be revived, make us living first!

### 3. Lord, raise up today’s Elijahs and Antipas; make even one person a contrary testimony

Lord Jesus, when the majority are lukewarm, You still call for overcomers. Elijah did not wait for the four hundred and fifty prophets to turn; Antipas did not wait for everyone to become faithful before he stood. O Lord, forgive us for using the coldness of others as an excuse and the failure of the majority as a covering. If others do not rise up, I will rise up; if others are unwilling, I will still consecrate myself. Even if I am the only one, I will stand for Your testimony. Lord, do not let us merely wait for overcomers to appear; make us the overcomers You need today. From one faithful witness, gain a company of vital companions; from one vital group, raise up a company of overcomers. No longer wait! Wake up, rise up, and become a contrary testimony in this age for the building up of the Body of Christ!

## Ministry Fellowship

### 1. The purpose of changing our practice is to change our concepts and our way of doing things

I would like to speak again with the brothers and sisters concerning the purpose of changing our practice. Negatively, it is to cause us truly to depart from traditional religion; positively, it is to recover the living and organic function of every member so that Christ and His Body may spread. … Therefore, in changing our practice today, the first thing is to change our concepts and our way of doing things. (*The Vision and Specific Steps of the Practice of the New Way*, message 5)

### 2. The change was a new beginning for the Lord to gain a definite model on the earth

At that time I was observing not only the Far East and Taiwan but also the West and the United States. I saw that the main reason the Lord could not move much among us was that a considerable amount of the poison of Christianity—its factors, elements, background, and environment—remained in us. Outwardly, we rejected the things of Christianity, but inwardly a considerable amount of its elements still remained. Therefore, I received a commission from the Lord to begin the change in Taiwan. In October 1984, as I was returning from the United States to Taiwan, I told the brothers there that this was not an ordinary visit. I was going back to make a new beginning, so that the Lord might gain a definite model on the earth and His entire recovery might enter into what is well pleasing to His heart. When I arrived in Taiwan, I began a thorough change. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1986, vol. 2, part A*)

### 3. Changing our practice requires three “willingnesses” and the filling of the Holy Spirit

The key is whether the elders are willing to enter in, willing to learn, and willing to practice. If they have these three willingnesses, the work will surely succeed. The elders take the lead; when they practice, the majority will follow. The elders must come in and lead the practice. I am not asking you to go door-knocking every day, but only once a week. I believe the Lord will bless your family. We do not lose anything by offering ourselves to the Lord. … Once the first few cars get on the road, the ones behind them can move too. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1986, vol. 3, part A*)

### 4. During the change in practice, Brother Lee was willing to wait for people to catch up

Today we want to take the new way and replace old practices with new ones. But remember that we should not change too quickly; if we do, people may fall behind and be unable to keep up. The Old Testament tells us that when Jacob was traveling with his many wives, children, and flocks to meet his brother, he knew they could not travel quickly. He said to his brother, “Let my lord pass on ahead of his servant, and I will proceed at my leisure, according to the pace of the livestock that are before me and according to the pace of the children” (Gen. 33:14). Jacob knew their strength and asked to move forward slowly. The church in Taichung was large, with several hundred people. If it moved too quickly, the older and younger ones might not be able to follow. Therefore, the change needed to be gradual, step by step. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1989, vol. 1*)

### 5. While changing our practice, we should bear with and wait for one another

We should be positive but not anxious. We all have an old background and foundation, and changing them for something new is not easy. We should not be hasty but should go on slowly. We need both a heart and patience. Those who have entered in should not despise those who have not, and those who have not entered in should not oppose those who have. We should love one another, bear with one another, and also wait for one another. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1989, vol. 2, part A*)

### 6. The churches may need a transition period of three years or more to enter fully into the new way

It may take three years or more for all the churches to enter fully into the new way. We must exercise wisdom and patience in the church where we are. Some have not yet practiced the truths concerning the scriptural way to meet and serve; do not impose anything on them. … If people do not listen, it is all right to go slowly. If they do not listen this week, speak again next week; if they do not listen this month, speak again next month. In any case, our tone should not change; our faces should be set like flint. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1987, vol. 3, part A*)

### 7. In 1989 his attitude began to turn: we do not need to wait for anyone; we should simply go

We do not need to wait for anyone; we should simply go. Some saints may say, “Preaching the gospel is good, but I do not feel that I should go.” They may not say this openly, but deep within they may have this feeling. So we do not need to wait for others. If we decide to wait for others, heaven and earth may pass away and they still will not take up the burden to preach the gospel. We should not wait. Instead, we should gather one or two people near us. We can find one younger person and one older person to go with us.

### 8. In 1992 he fellowshipped that one vital saint could begin practicing the vital groups

The following outline is entitled “How to Produce and Establish the Vital Groups in the Church Life.” We hope that we will read and study this outline prayerfully so that we may become vital and go forth to make others vital.

I. By one saint becoming vital—living and active:

1. By continually having a thorough, clear, and complete fellowship with the Lord.
2. By thoroughly confessing sins, transgressions, failures, shortcomings, mistakes, and faults, whether hidden or manifested and whether before God or before man.
3. By fully and thoroughly consecrating ourselves and everything to the Lord.
4. By praying unceasingly and desperately.
5. By being saturated with the inward filling and the outward outpouring of the Spirit.

II. By contacting another seeking saint:

1. Fellowship with him or her according to the above procedure.
2. Make him or her vital and join him or her to you to become a group.
3. Continue to contact other seeking saints, make them vital, and join them to you, so that your group may increase. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1991–1992, vol. 3, part A*)

### 9. In 1993 he urged the saints to rise up and become vital

In the place where you are, many may be living a routine church life. All those living such a routine church life are defeated; they are not overcomers. But you must rise up and take the vital way. Then in the church where you are, you will be a vital person who can produce and establish vital groups. The Lord will gain some overcomers there and have a way to accomplish His eternal economy. (*The Training and the Practice of the Vital Groups*, introduction)

### 10. If we keep waiting instead of contacting people, we will never become vital

I believe the way to help us become more vital is to contact people. If we keep waiting and do not contact people, we will never become vital. Each week we should set aside one day to contact people. In addition to the regular meetings of the church, we also need to set aside one evening each week for a vital group meeting. We should still attend the Lord’s Day morning meeting and the prayer meeting. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1991–1992, vol. 3, part A*)

### 11. When the fire is kindled in the right place, the whole house will catch fire

If you want to burn a house, you must kindle the fire in the right place. You only need to pour a little gasoline in that place and set it on fire, and the whole house will burn. To be burning for the Lord and vital all the time, we also need to return to the right place. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1993, vol. 2, part A*)

### 12. The 2022 Summer Training: when the majority are lukewarm, the overcomers bear a contrary testimony

Elijah represents a principle: he did not care for his own life but for the maintaining of God’s testimony. He stood strongly and boldly before the king, the people, and the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal. When the church is degraded and the majority are lukewarm, God raises up the overcomers to be a contrary testimony. They do not care for their own lives but only for God’s will—as with “Antipas, My witness, My faithful one, who was killed among you, where Satan dwells” (Rev. 2:13–14; 12:11). (*2022 Summer Training, message 2*)
