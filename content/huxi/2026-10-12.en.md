---
title: For family and friends around us
---

Today we bring the people around us before the Lord together.

- For family members who do not yet believe: name them one by one, and ask the Lord to open their hearts.
- For the brothers and sisters reading together this week: that the Lord would keep their time and give them an appetite for the Word.
- For students: the Lord's presence and peace in the middle of the pressure of school.
- For every article and video shared from this site: that the Lord would use them.

> Ask and it shall be given to you; seek and you shall find; knock and it shall be opened to you.
> —— Matthew 7:7 (Recovery Version)
