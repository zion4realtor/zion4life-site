## Today’s Prayer Burden

From one or two hundred meetings a year, may we advance to daily practice, weekly blending and fellowship, new fruit every month, reproduction every quarter, and propagation year by year. Lord, lead us from breakthrough to breakthrough and onward step by step. May every vital group have a small revival each quarter and become a living platform for a corporate company of Nehemiahs to reproduce and multiply, bringing in the hundredfold increase in five years that Brother Lee fellowshipped about during the coming decade.

## Today’s Prayer

### 1. Lord, make us people who can reproduce others

Lord Jesus, bring us from “I can do it myself” to “I can help others learn to do it and reproduce it.” You are the first God-man, the prototype, the pattern, and the mold. We do not come merely to imitate You; we open to Your living word so that You Yourself may be reproduced in us. Lord, constitute the new ones in the home training day by day in the revelation of the truth, the practice of life, gospel shepherding, and coordination in function. Make them more than people who are cared for; make them Your reproduction, able to reproduce others. Lead them forward stage by stage: new ones becoming helpers, helpers becoming householders, and householders becoming companions in a corporate company of Nehemiahs.

### 2. Lord, open our eyes to the multiplying power of reproduction

Lord Jesus, open our eyes to see how great the multiplying power within reproduction is. Begin with a vital group of five companions. If each one reproduces just one companion in a year who can continue reproducing others, there will be about 160 after five years. If each person reproduces an average of one and a half such companions a year, there will be about 488 after five years. If each person reproduces two, there will be 1,215 after five years.

### 3. Lord, make us practice one begetting one, one generation producing another

Companions, the figures help us see the potential of the life reproduction ordained by God: “one begetting one, one generation producing another.” Brother Lee fellowshipped that if all 250,000 saints practiced the vital groups, their number could become 500,000 in one year and one million the next. He also testified that at the beginning of the work in Taiwan, the saints were full of vitality and everyone functioned; in only five years there was an astonishing hundredfold increase.

### 4. Lord, shepherd and perfect the new ones so they can continue reproducing

Lord, shake us to see that what truly limits the increase is not a shortage of gospel contacts but a shortage of people who have been reproduced. It is not that the new ones cannot function; it is that we have not shepherded them until they can reproduce others. Their inability to reproduce is also related to our failure to continue leading them forward stage by stage after the first three months of home training. Yes, Lord, may the new ones become helpers, the helpers become householders, and the householders become companions in a corporate company of Nehemiahs, one stage after another. Do not let us be satisfied with meeting attendance, baptism numbers, or our own labor. Make us diligently nourish, perfect, and hand over each new one, until a substantial portion can repeat what we have done and then gain and perfect the next generation.

### 5. Lord, make us practice daily and reproduce year by year

Lord Jesus, lead our practice from one day to one week, from one week to one month, from one month to one quarter, from one quarter to one year, from one year to ten years—winning a thousand years in ten. We make a serious consecration to You: recover among us the solid, earnest, vital, and continual practice of reproducing the most basic aspects of the church life. From one or two hundred meetings a year, advance to daily practice, weekly blending and fellowship, new fruit every month, reproduction every quarter, and propagation year by year. Lead us from breakthrough to breakthrough and onward step by step. May every vital group have a small revival each quarter and enjoy the living platform of a corporate company of Nehemiahs for reproduction and multiplication, bringing in the hundredfold increase in five years that Brother Lee fellowshipped about in our present day.

### 6. Lord, let the reproduction of life build up the Body

Lord, show us that reproduction is not extra work; it is the result of the maturity of life. When new ones reproduce new ones, the vital groups are truly living. As one generation continually reproduces another, the churches have an organic increase. Bring this revived scene into reality among us until Your Body is built up and Your bride is prepared. Amen.

## Ministry Fellowship

### He is the model and prototype of a God-man, producing many God-men

The God-man is Jesus Christ. God became incarnated to be a man; as such, He is a God-man. Moreover, He is the model and prototype of a God-man, producing many God-men (Rom. 1:3–4; 8:29), including all the dear saints throughout the world. Whatever our nationality, whether male or female, young or old, we must believe that we are God-men. We are a people of God-men; each of us is a God-man. (*Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1994–1997, vol. 2, part A*)

### He is the pattern and mold; we are His mass production, Christ’s reproduction and propagation

We are copies of Christ, and these copies are a mingling of the Divine Trinity with humanity. He is the pattern and mold; we are His mass production. Hymn 154, stanzas 2 and 4, expresses the thought that we are Christ’s reproduction and propagation:

Through death and resurrection You
became God’s Firstborn Son;
By regeneration, life imparted,
we became God’s many sons.
We are the reproduction of Your life,
Your many brothers;
we are Your reproduction in glory,
Your divine enlargement. (*Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1991–1992, vol. 1, part A*)

### Jesus is the living pattern

Praise the Lord, in the four Gospels we can see the living, original pattern. As we read them, we see Jesus as this living pattern. This may tempt us to try to imitate Him. When we read that Jesus was meek, we try to be meek, but we never succeed; even if we do, our meekness is not genuine. We need to open ourselves to the deepest part of our being and realize that neither our flesh nor our mind is our real person. Our real person is our hidden spirit. There is indeed a hidden part deep within us: our human spirit, the innermost part of our being. Under the shining of the living word, we must open and unveil our hidden spirit to Jesus. The living word of the Bible is firm, and we must pay attention to it. When we open to a chapter in one of the four Gospels, our spirit should be open to the Lord to pray-read that chapter. As we pray-read the Lord’s word, the verses become a lamp shining in a dark place. The more we open to the living word in this way, the more Jesus, the original pattern, is unveiled to us, and the more our deepest part is opened to Him. The result is that a part of Jesus is reproduced in us. This is not imitation; it is the reproduction of Jesus. (*Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1973–1974, vol. 1, part A*)

### The members of the vital groups are trained to live the God-man life according to Christ, the first God-man

As members of the vital groups, we must remember and experience that we are God’s regenerated new creation, joined to Christ in His divine life and united with Him as one spirit (2 Cor. 5:17; 1 Cor. 6:17). … We are being trained from a natural person to become a God-man, denying our natural life and living the divine life according to the example of Christ, the first God-man (Matt. 28:19). … We should live according to Christ, the model of the first God-man. When Christ was on earth, He denied His natural life, which was Himself. He said that the words He spoke were not His own but those of the Father who sent Him (John 14:24). He never did anything from Himself (5:19, 30). In everything He did, He acted from and by the Father who sent Him. He was not the Sender but the sent One. He did not live Himself; He lived the Father who sent Him (6:57a). This is the model of the first God-man. (*Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1994–1997, vol. 5, part A*)

### Each person reproduces one each year, and the new ones do the same

Genesis 1 says that after God created man, He blessed him and told him to be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth (v. 28). God’s way, however, is gradual: one begets one. A woman can bear only once a year, and a child must be conceived and carried for nine months before birth. After six thousand years, the whole earth was filled with people. Therefore, in preaching the gospel we should follow the principle ordained by God. We should not trust in large gospel meetings; we have learned that they are not effective. Someone may say, “Brother Lee, didn’t you hold large gospel meetings more than forty years ago?” At that time I was preparing people. After each meeting, I gave the lists of names to the saints, who visited the people one by one. We should not keep holding large meetings; instead, we should practice the vital groups and go out to knock on doors and visit people one by one. If a church has only 250 people and everyone practices the vital groups, one begetting one would mean 500 the following year. If all 250,000 people in the Lord’s recovery practiced the vital groups, there would be 500,000 after one year and one million after the next. It may seem slow, but it is actually fast. The vital groups are the proper practice; there is no need to establish a seminary or hold large meetings. (*Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1994–1997, vol. 5, part A*)

### The testimony of a hundredfold increase in the first five years of the work in Taiwan

At the beginning of the work in Taiwan, the situation was different: the saints were full of vitality, and everyone functioned. As a result, the increase in the first year was thirtyfold. The first year was thirtyfold; by the fourth or fifth year it was a hundredfold. In only a few years, the number grew from several hundred to tens of thousands. At that time there were large meetings, meetings in branch meeting halls, groups within the meeting halls, and leading ones over the groups. By 1980, however, only a few people in each church remained who could give messages. (*The Mystery of the Universe and the Meaning of Human Life*, message 9)

### At least half of those we shepherd can become our reproduction and reproduce new ones

After the new believers have been nourished and perfected in this way for a year, we can encourage them to go out and do the same things we have done, repeating what we have done just as we gained them. At least half of those under our care can, within a year, attain the measure and qualification to do the same things we do—to bear fruit that remains. Year after year another generation will be born and perfected. (*1989, vol. 1, part A*)
