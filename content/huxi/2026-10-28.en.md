After thirty-five years of sowing in Europe, the coming decade will be a crucial time for the Lord’s move there. May the Lord prepare the situation of the churches in Europe through a new pattern of church life, with all the saints growing in life, being knit together, and functioning. ## Ministry Fellowship

### 1. Thirty-five years of sowing (1991–2026)

Before March 1991, I had not thought that the Lord’s move needed a new direction. But after the war between the United Nations coalition and Iraq, I was deeply impressed. The recent battle with Iraq lasted only four days and four hours, a total of one hundred hours. After that battle I reconsidered the world situation. We must consider what the Lord wants us to do today. The Lord’s move in His recovery today needs an appropriate direction to match the recent changes in the world situation.

In recent years, while we have been changing to practice the church life according to the God-ordained way, the world has also been changing. In view of the present world situation, the Lord’s move in His recovery today needs an appropriate direction. We should not think that the recent changes in the world situation are meaningless. The Lord is not only the Creator of the universe; He is also the Ruler of the whole world.

Everything that happens in the world situation is of the Lord. When we see the many changes in today’s world situation, we should wake up. Our eyes need to be opened, and we should exercise our spirit to say, “Lord, what is Your intention in all these changes? What do they have to do with us?”

Recently several brothers went to Eastern Europe and the Soviet Union. They testified that the people there were open to the divine truth and exceedingly hungry for it. Through our contact with people in Leipzig, about eight received the Lord. In Berlin the brothers contacted a Russian-speaking woman, and she was baptized. She was our first fruit in East Berlin. After praying with the brothers to receive the Lord, she invited them to dinner at her home, and that very evening they baptized her. This surely confirmed the openness there and testified that a door had been opened for the Lord’s move in Europe.

The Lord’s recovery is growing in the Far East and in the United States, but in Europe it is still in its initial stage. We should remember that the United States, Europe, and the Far East are the three major factors influencing the present world situation. The Lord’s recovery has taken root in the United States and the Far East, but Europe remains an empty space. For this reason, the present direction of the Lord’s recovery must be toward Europe. The recovery there is still in its initial stage.

#### The importance of Europe

Europe is extremely important to the present world situation because of its geographical position, its people, its history and culture, and its measure of capability. Furthermore, in the ultimate fulfillment of the vision of the great human image in Daniel 2, Europe is more crucial and important than any other nation or race. The striking of the two feet of the great image is the striking of the entire human government (Dan. 2:34–35).

We need to see this as a basis for understanding the Lord’s intention. Before human government is smashed, the Lord’s recovery must spread to Europe and take root there. The spreading of the truth of the Lord’s recovery will prepare the way for the Lord’s return and bring in the restoration and revival of Israel and the entire creation.

Of the three factors influencing today’s world situation, the Far East and the United States have already been occupied and gained by the Lord’s recovery. Europe, however, still needs the Lord’s recovery to take root and grow there. I hope we will bring this fellowship to the Lord in prayer. We should say to Him, “Lord, these days are the consummation of this age. Rekindle my love for You in these days.”

(Witness Lee, *The World Situation and the Direction of the Lord’s Move*, ch. 1.)

### 3. A new pattern of church life to meet the world situation and prepare for the Lord’s coming

#### Brother Lee’s foresight: the closer the Lord’s coming, the more the church returns to its original condition

Brother Lee saw what the church would be like before the Lord’s return. He saw farther than all of us: the church would gradually and slowly return to its original condition. What was the church like originally? It was like the church in the first century: there were no large meetings, only small meetings. The closer the Lord’s coming, the more the church will return to its original condition.

#### Treasure each member and build a personal relationship with each one

First, our concept must be turned around completely. We must see that the most important thing is each member, each person, and we must build a personal relationship with each one. Building a personal relationship with people requires being “small”; without being small, we cannot build personal relationships. If you want to care for someone where he is, why do you ask him to come to you? Actually, you are asking him to support your gathering. If you no longer had the gathering, would you still ask him to come? For example, if the government stopped you from holding a large gathering, or if you yourself felt that the gathering was not the issue and was unnecessary, you would no longer ask people to come; you might even give up asking them to come, and then there would be no question of “coming.” It is actually very difficult to get people to come to a meeting. Therefore, our concept is crucial. This is a matter of turning our concept around!

#### The old system is built on meetings; the new system is built on relationships

The old system is built on “meetings,” whereas the new system is built on “relationships.” For example, when a church builds organic relationships, it is like a neural network, with a first circle, then a second and a third. The homes, groups, and districts may care for the same people or for different people; the sisters can also participate. At least half our time should be spent building and strengthening these organic relationships. It would not be too much to spend all our time on this.

I say that Brother Lee had foresight in leading the Lord’s recovery to take the new way. He did not see only that the church needed to increase and the number of believers needed to multiply. He also saw what the world situation would be like before the Lord’s coming and that the church must move in this direction.

(Brother Yu, “In the End Time, Practicing the New Way and Building a New Pattern of Church Life,” February 11, 2020.)
